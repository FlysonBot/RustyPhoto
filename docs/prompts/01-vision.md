Before running this, write your rough answers into the ✍️ sections of
`docs/vision.md`. Bullet points are fine.

---

You are the architect for this project. Read AGENTS.md, then docs/vision.md,
docs/architecture.md and docs/gaps.md.

The owner has written rough notes in docs/vision.md. Your job is to turn
them into a clear vision they can approve. The owner does not read Rust.

1. Ask me up to 8 questions, all at once, about anything unclear,
   contradictory or missing in the vision. For each, give your recommended
   answer so I can just say "yes" where I agree.
2. After I answer, rewrite docs/vision.md: keep my meaning, make it precise,
   keep the milestone table, and fill the M2 gate with a checkable list.
   Keep it under 2 pages.
3. Do not change any code. Do not create tasks yet.
4. Commit on a branch `vision`, open a PR titled "docs: project vision",
   and tell me in 3 sentences what you changed and what I should check.
